# Xean: The First AI-Native Property Management Platform

Property management software hasn't changed much in a decade. Spreadsheets. PDFs nobody reads twice. A shoebox of maintenance requests that all sound urgent until someone actually calls the tenant back. Late-night phone calls that go straight to voicemail. Move-in inspections photographed on a phone and never seen again — until there's a dispute six months later and nobody can find them.

We built Xean because we were tired of it. In 2026, Xean is the first property management platform built AI-native from the ground up — not a legacy system with a chatbot bolted on, but software where artificial intelligence sits at the center of how the work actually gets done.

## Why "AI-native" is not a marketing word here

A lot of software claims to use AI. Most of it means a search bar that summarizes things, or a chatbot wedged into the corner of an otherwise unchanged product. That's not what we mean.

At Xean, AI is the thing that reads your leases, triages your maintenance requests, talks to your tenants at 2am, and figures out what a repair actually needs — before a human ever has to get involved. Here's what that looks like in practice, today, not on a roadmap slide.

### Leases read themselves

Upload a lease PDF, and Xean's AI extracts the tenant's name, rent, deposit, and lease dates automatically — tested and confirmed accurate against real documents, no retyping, no manual data entry. What used to be twenty minutes of copying numbers from a PDF into a spreadsheet is now a five-second upload.

### Maintenance requests triage themselves — and now they can see

When a tenant reports an issue, Xean's AI reads it, figures out the trade category and urgency, and gets it in front of the right person immediately. But we went further: the AI can now genuinely see what a tenant sends. Attach a photo of a leaking pipe or a cracked outlet, and the AI actually looks at it — recognizing what's really wrong, not just guessing from a text description. If a photo would help and none has been sent, the AI will naturally ask for one, the same way a good property manager would.

### A conversation before a ticket, when it's safe to have one

Not every maintenance issue needs a manager at 11pm. Xean's AI will walk a tenant through basic, safe troubleshooting first — checking a breaker switch, a shutoff valve, a GFCI reset — before a real ticket ever gets created. It knows exactly where the line is: never electrical panels, never gas lines, never anything requiring a ladder or specialized tools. When in doubt, it defaults to getting a human involved rather than guessing. And if a tenant flags something as an emergency at any point, the AI stops troubleshooting immediately and alerts the property manager right away.

### Every conversation, in the tenant's own language

Xean speaks English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Mandarin, and Arabic — with full right-to-left layout support for Arabic, not just translated buttons. A tenant chatting with Xean's AI about a gas smell gets told, in their own language, to leave immediately and call emergency services — the same safety-first response, in six languages, verified to hold up in every one of them.

### A real ledger, not a spreadsheet

Rent, deposits, add-on charges like parking or pet fees, late fees, credits — all tracked in a genuine running ledger with automatic monthly billing. No more reconstructing who owes what from a stack of e-transfers. Property managers can generate a full payment history as a PDF in seconds, whether it's for a tenant's tax records or their own bookkeeping.

### Move-in inspections that don't disappear

Room-by-room condition reports, photographed and documented, tied directly to the tenant's file — with the tenant able to review and sign off on it themselves, from their own portal. No more searching a phone's camera roll for photos from eight months ago.

### A tenant portal from day one

Every tenant gets a real account — their lease, their documents, their inspection report, their payment ledger, their maintenance history, all in one place from the moment they move in. Not a shared inbox. Not a filing cabinet. Their own portal, installable as an app on their phone.

### A maintenance team that actually gets dispatched

Property managers can build a maintenance team directly in Xean and assign tickets to specific people. Each team member gets their own login, sees only the jobs assigned to them, can message the manager directly — photos, videos, and product links included — and has to file a completion note before a job can be marked done. No more "did someone actually fix that?"

## Built for how property managers actually work

We didn't design Xean around what a big enterprise software company imagines property management looks like from a boardroom. We built it around the reality of running a portfolio — where you're wearing five hats, most requests come in outside business hours, and the tools you use need to just work, not require a training manual.

That's why Xean installs like a real app on your phone — for managers and tenants alike — and works even with a spotty connection. It's why the AI defaults to caution on anything safety-related, every time, in every language. It's why a manager can search across an entire portfolio by name, email, or phone number in seconds, and pull a real analytics report — who's behind on rent, how many days, how many units sit vacant — as a clean PDF or spreadsheet.
